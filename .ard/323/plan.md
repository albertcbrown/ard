### Risk

Mitigations are the single tree invariant enforced in the committer, no git in the agent's worktree after the agent step, a strict private-ref `MergeParent` with no merge-mode retry, and hardened merge flags. Public interfaces: `committer.CodeRequest`, `gitx.CommitOp`, `effects`, `gitx.Git`, `servecmd` wiring. No migration. No `merge.protected_paths` entry is edited. The switch turns on before the human-written worker prompt section lands unless that PR merges first or alongside.
